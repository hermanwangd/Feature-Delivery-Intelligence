# Publication Policy

Allowed policy labels:

- `HUMAN_APPROVAL`
- `RULE_BASED_AUTO`
- `SOURCE_REFERENCE`

Structural deterministic facts may be eligible for rule-based publication only when exact pinned evidence, provider binding, schema validation and authority rules all pass.

Semantic interpretations such as Product/SubProduct/Capability classification, business rules, durable invariants or ambiguous ownership default to `HUMAN_APPROVAL`.

No Agent observation silently mutates a published Product Asset.
