# PA-03 / PA-05 Boundary

- **PA-03 Codebase:** repository inventory and high-value reusable navigation relations. Relations are incomplete/qualified navigation intelligence, not a complete enterprise dependency graph.
- **PA-05 Delivery History:** reusable historical delivery records with explicit correlation/provenance. Historical patterns generate/prioritize candidates; they do not establish current feature applicability.

> PA-03 says where the product is. PA-05 says where similar changes went before. FT-T2 establishes what the current feature actually requires.
