## Compatibility

The candidate reuses the approved Layer 2 Product Asset descriptor and assigns
only Product/Sub-product/Capability semantics. It explicitly leaves repository
realization to PA-03, historical delivery to PA-05, and current feature truth to
feature-specific pinned Evidence. These boundaries are compatible with the
approved v0.1 framework/profile set.

Promotion requires a separate Human approval reference. Until then, no
`normative/layer2/profiles/PA-01-product-semantics-v0.1` module is created and no
baseline may select PA-01 as approved.
